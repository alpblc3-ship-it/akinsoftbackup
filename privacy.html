Privacy Policy

AkinsoftBackup runs on the user's own computer and uploads the user's backup files directly to the user's own Google Drive account.

Data we access

The app uses the Google Drive "drive.file" scope. It can only access files and folders that it created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Data we collect or share

We do not collect, store, sell or share your data. The app has no server. Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on your own computer, and backup files go only from your computer to your Google Drive.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
